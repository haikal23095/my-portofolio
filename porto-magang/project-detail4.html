Overview

This project involves the development of a web-based information system designed specifically to support the operational management needs of a hardware store. The web application encompasses various key operational modules, such as Item Management, Supplier Management, Stock Management, Point of Sale (POS), Cash Flow Recording, and monthly report generation.

Key Features

  • Master Data Management
  • Cashflow Management
  • Report Management
