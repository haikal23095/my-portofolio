Overview

The Edoc Application is used to handle correspondence business processes within the faculty campus environment. It handles various business processes ranging from applications for active student certificates, internship recommendation letters, faculty decree (SK) for lecturers, etc. This application is a replication of business processes within the Faculty of Engineering, Trunojoyo University Madura.
