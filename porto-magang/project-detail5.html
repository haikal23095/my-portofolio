Overview

The Mini-ERP System is a micro-scale Enterprise Resource Planning application specifically designed to support operational efficiency for e-commerce stores—particularly digital marketplaces like Shopee, TikTok Shop, and Tokopedia, as well as offline and WhatsApp-based sales. The application features multi-store data isolation and integrates raw material supply chain management, daily employee assembly and production processes, and the calculation of True Net Profit.

Key Features
  • Multi-Store Data Isolation
  • Product Catalog & BOM (Products & BOM)
  • Raw Materials & Stock Purchases
  • Daily Production & Assembly
  • Sales Records (Multi-Channel)
  • Expense & Cash Flow Management
